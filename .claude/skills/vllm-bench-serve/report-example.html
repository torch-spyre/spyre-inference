PR #XXX vs main: decode optimization

PR #XXX optimizes decode by batching the per-sequence KV gathers into one kernel. On granite-3.3-8b at TP4 with the 8k cics trace, output throughput rises 27% and mean ITL falls 22%. Median and P99 TTFT are flat, but mean TTFT is slightly worse (+4%) and P100 TTFT regresses 18%.

Throughput and ITL move by about the same amount, as expected when each decode step gets shorter at fixed concurrency. P100 ITL hardly changes (−1.3%), and the TTFT regression is confined to the tail: the slowest prefill is 18% slower while the median and P99 are unchanged. Worth checking whether the PR's decode path delays a prefill that lands in a busy batch.
